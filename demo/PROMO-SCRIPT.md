# Osteon · Make anatomy click

111-second feature tour · November 4, 2026

Warm conversational female voice. Qwen3-TTS generated locally in ComfyUI. Original ACE-Step instrumental beneath the narration.

## 0:00 · Make anatomy click.

Your next anatomy exam deserves more than flat diagrams. Meet Osteon: a free study space that makes anatomy click.

## 0:09 · Explore in three dimensions.

In the 3D atlas, drag to rotate, and scroll or pinch to zoom. Double-click or double-tap to explore a region, then again to focus one bone. Back restores context.

## 0:24 · Muscles. In your current view.

Tap Muscles to add muscle layers without losing your view. Choose a region, select a muscle, and use Peel to look beneath covering surfaces.

## 0:36 · Find the details that matter.

Turn on Landmarks for clear markers. Select one to reveal its name, then choose Show best view to inspect the target.

## 0:46 · Turn recognition into recall.

Open Practice Lab and name the highlighted structure. Clear spelling slips can keep credit, while feedback helps you separate terminology from concept gaps.

## 0:58 · Understand. Then test yourself.

Study Studio connects OpenStax chapters six through ten with guided lessons, source readings, and interactive activities. Choose a chapter exam or mixed review, then save and resume later.

## 1:13 · A nudge. Not just an answer.

Need a nudge? With Ollama running on your computer, choose an installed model in Socratic Tutor. Ask a question and work through the reasoning, one step at a time.

## 1:27 · Make your next session count.

My Progress turns practice history into focused review. Revisit weak spots, and take your studying from desktop to phone.

## 1:37 · Free. Student-built. Always growing.

Built by an anatomy and physiology student as he learns, Osteon keeps growing with new content and improvements. Free to open. No account needed. Try it today, and share it with your class.
