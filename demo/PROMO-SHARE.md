# Share Osteon with your A&P class

## Class-group post

A&P classmates: make anatomy click with Osteon 🦴 Explore a 3D skeleton and muscle layers, practice anatomical names with spelling support, and work through OpenStax chapters 6–10 with guided lessons and exams. Optional local Socratic tutoring uses Ollama on your computer. Built by an A&P student as he learns, and always growing with new content and improvements. Free in your browser—no account needed. Try it and share it with your study group: https://rorrimaesu.github.io/osteon-study/

## Files

- `osteon-promo.mp4`: 111-second widescreen feature tour and tutorial, 1920 × 1080.
- `osteon-promo-social.mp4`: 35-second portrait teaser, 1080 × 1920.
- Both videos include warm female narration, original instrumental music and burned-in captions for silent viewing. Separate `.vtt` caption files are provided.
- `promo-poster.jpg`, `promo-social-poster.jpg`: matching covers.
- `promo-preview.gif`: nine-second animated preview.
- `PROMO-SCRIPT.md`: full narration transcript.
- `PROMO-PRODUCTION.md`: research, revisions and checks.

Upload the portrait MP4 for a Story or Reel, or attach the full tour to a class-group post. Include the clickable app link in the post; the link displayed inside a video cannot be tapped. A story link sticker can point to the app when the platform supports it. Optional AI needs separate Ollama setup on a computer; the atlas, study and practice tools work without it.
