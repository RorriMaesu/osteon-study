Full narration transcript and how it was made
  1. Meet Osteon, your anatomy and physiology study space.
  2. Drag to rotate. Scroll to zoom. Double-click a bone to explore its region; double-click again to focus on that bone. Use Back to return.
  3. Switch between the axial and appendicular skeletons. Turn regional colors on to compare neighboring bones.
  4. Here is the foramen magnum: the large opening in the occipital bone. Hide the label, then test yourself with a numbered target and check your answer.
  5. Need a nudge? Open the Socratic tutor and choose an installed Ollama model. Ask for one question at a time. It uses your current study context and links to the supplied textbook readings.
  6. Then connect what you see with OpenStax chapters six and seven in the study studio.
  7. Explore, identify, and return to what needs practice, on desktop or phone. Try Osteon free in your browser. No account needed. Local tutoring requires Ollama.

The vertical edit shows phone layouts for exploration, the same opening, practice, tutor feedback, and chapter study. These are genuine browser captures from an isolated demonstration session. The base atlas, chapter study and practice work without Ollama. Optional tutoring requires Ollama and a local chat model on your computer.
