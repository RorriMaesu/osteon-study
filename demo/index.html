Demo transcript and how it was made
  1. Explore: a double-click on the full skeleton opens the skull region.
  2. Rotate: the regional skull turns, showing the neighboring bones in color.
  3. Locate: the nasolacrimal canal is labeled on a bone reconstruction; the label is switched off.
  4. Recall: an identification exercise hides the name. “Nasolacrimal canal” is entered and receives correct feedback.
  5. Study: the study studio opens the chapter 6 bone-structure lesson.
