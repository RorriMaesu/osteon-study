# Osteon narrated tour

Voice: warm conversational adult female, American English. Generated locally with Qwen3-TTS 1.7B in ComfyUI.

## 01-intro

Meet Osteon, your anatomy and physiology study space.

## 02-controls

Drag to rotate. Scroll to zoom. Double-click a bone to explore its region; double-click again to focus on that bone. Use Back to return.

## 03-divisions

Switch between the axial and appendicular skeletons. Turn regional colors on to compare neighboring bones.

## 04-landmark

Here is the foramen magnum: the large opening in the occipital bone. Hide the label, then test yourself with a numbered target and check your answer.

## 05-tutor

Need a nudge? Open the Socratic tutor and choose an installed Ollama model. Ask for one question at a time. It uses your current study context and links to the supplied textbook readings.

## 06-study

Then connect what you see with OpenStax chapters six and seven in the study studio.

## 07-close

Explore, identify, and return to what needs practice, on desktop or phone. Try Osteon free in your browser. No account needed. Local tutoring requires Ollama.
