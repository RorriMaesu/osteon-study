    How to read this inventory

    The 380 checks cover named skeletal muscles and contractile subdivisions in FIPAT’s muscular-system chapter, plus a separate clinical check for puborectalis. The inventory also tracks all 61 course targets.

    A static match identifies a named surface in the atlas. Walk context moves with the body and may use a guided blend. Study motions show specific joint movement or recorded activity. Each row shows the evidence for its individual parts.
