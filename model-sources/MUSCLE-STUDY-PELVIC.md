# Pelvic study models

## Ischiocavernosus and pelvic diaphragm

The original male teaching model is **Open3DAnatomy — Pelvic Floor and Perineum** by Marco C. DeRuiter, Eungyeol Lee, Daniël Jansma, Paul Gobée and collaborators at Leiden University Medical Center, with credit to BodyParts3D and Z-Anatomy. The original and the two adapted study exports are available under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). [Leiden source model](https://caskanatomy.info/open3dviewer/?model=pelvicfloor) · [Original GLB](https://caskanatomy.info/open3dviewer/3dmodels/pelvicfloor/pelvicfloor.glb) · [Model project and license](https://anatomytool.org/content/open3danatomy-3d-model-pelvic-floor-and-perineum-english-labels).

- **Ischiocavernosus.** The source object `Ischiocavernosus muscle.r` contains two pre-existing symmetric muscle surfaces, one on either side of the source midsagittal plane. Osteon isolated the pair, welded seams, removed duplicate coincident faces, applied a study color, and centered it. Both components are closed after cleanup and retain 426 triangles each.
- **Pelvic diaphragm.** Named donor surfaces supply the right coccygeus, right iliococcygeus and combined pubococcygeus–puborectalis surface. The donor's separately named pubococcygeus and puborectalis overlays mark their relative locations on the combined muscle surface. Osteon mirrors the right-hand source components across the midsagittal plane and preserves these two overlays as surface regions. Coccygeus is blue, iliococcygeus is clay, puborectalis is mint and pubococcygeus is gold. This study export omits the surrounding organs, anal sphincter and other perineal muscles.

The original file was read only and has SHA-256 `3846fc5c8f2db944a174648a8cdfe91ca1a6ec16d7d54389d585e69d7b1f7e92`. The [Blender 4.5 extraction script](../scripts/blender/build_leiden_pelvic_study.py) verifies that fingerprint and writes into a new destination. Osteon reimported the exports in Blender 4.5.1 and reviewed upper, lower or side views of each model. The original atlas GLBs are untouched.

## External urethral sphincter

The female sphincter model is a new **teaching schematic** of the striated sphincter proper. Osteon adapted the annotated skeletal muscle boundaries from figure 2c, 2f and 2i of Tong Liu, Satoru Muro, Suthasinee Tharnmanularp and Keiichi Akita (2025), *Three-dimensional Analysis of the Distribution of Smooth and Skeletal Muscle Tissue Around the Female Urethra*. The schematic retains [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). [Histology and source reconstruction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12003430/#Fig2).

The [build script](../scripts/build_urethral_sphincter_study.py) uses simplified contours at 11, 18 and 21 mm from the urethrovesical junction. It uses the lumen as a common center, reflects the outlines for a symmetric display, and interpolates and smooths the surfaces. The urethral passage remains open and the middle cross-section opens posteriorly. The distal anterior separation near 21 mm follows the published section. The model shows the sphincter proper in isolation. The larger external sphincter complex, surrounding smooth-muscle layers, urethra and male form require their own source geometry. This study approximation uses no original specimen segmentation and provides no clinical measurements.
