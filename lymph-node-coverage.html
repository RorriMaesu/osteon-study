OSTEON / 3D ATLAS

Lymph-node coverage

This atlas shows representative adult lymph-node groups. Some source meshes contain several nodes, and the arrangement of individual nodes varies. Several left and right meshes are mirrored in the source.

Position checked means the mesh passed a placement check against nearby modeled structures. Source position means the original mesh is available for exploration and its individual anatomical review is pending. Withheld means the mesh stays out of the viewer while its placement is reviewed.

Use the OpenStax chapter section to learn the big picture and the HuBMAP anatomy map to study drainage relationships. Cross-checks used the Visible Human node atlas, the cervical level consensus, the axillary cadaver study, the popliteal dissection study the IASLC thoracic map, the inguinal cadaveric addendum and abdominal CT station descriptions.
