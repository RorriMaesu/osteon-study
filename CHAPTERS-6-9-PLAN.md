# Chapters 6–9: teaching and assessment plan

Source: the supplied *OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e*, © 2026 Rice University. The four chapters occupy PDF pages 213–384 (printed pages 197–368). Access for free at openstax.org. Source material and adaptations retain CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 attribution.

## Learning design

The aim is to explain skeletal structure and function, identify anatomy, distinguish close alternatives, and apply mechanisms to unfamiliar examples. Reading, self-rated familiarity, assisted answers, and independent exam performance are recorded separately.

Each section follows the same sequence:

1. Orient: show prerequisites and every source learning objective.
2. Study: concise teaching notes, distinctions, and the complete source text and figures.
3. Locate or model: use the atlas, a labeled source figure, or a guided process activity as appropriate.
4. Retrieve: answer before revealing an explanation; verbal explanations are self-checks, not automatically scored evidence.
5. Apply: predict an outcome and compare the reasoning with a model response.
6. Assess: mixed-format section practice, followed by a chapter exam.
7. Revisit: return to missed or assisted concepts; use a later cumulative exam to test transfer.

## Assessment design

- Four chapter exams: **50 questions each**, with the section allocations above.
- Cumulative exam: **100 questions**, **25 from each chapter**, with every section and objective represented.
- Full-bank review: all 400 authored exam-bank items (100 per chapter), with save/resume. Short practice is explicitly a sample.
- Formats: multiple choice, controlled typed recall, anatomical source-figure and schematic identification, matching and sequence ordering. Application reasoning is assessed through explicit problems and explanations. Free explanations in lessons remain self-checks.
- Each question cites a textbook section and names the learning objective it actually tests. A coverage check rejects missing objectives or an impossible blueprint rather than silently shortening the exam.
- Questions and choices are shuffled once per saved session. Exam answers are editable and flaggable until submission. Feedback is delayed until submission; practice gives immediate feedback.
- All-or-nothing marking gives one point per question, including matching and ordering. Exact accepted synonyms are disclosed after submission. Built-in, question-specific spelling and equivalent-wording support preserves clear intended answers. Optional local AI may review uncertain wording; uncertainty remains pending. Original answers, grading versions and AI evidence are preserved.
- Hints, source reading and tutor use mark an affected response as assisted. Results distinguish content accuracy from independent correctness. A guessed or recalled answer does not establish clinical competence.
- Reports show chapter, section and objective coverage; missed/assisted retries have their own denominators. Attempt records preserve existing study history.

## Critique and limits

Full readings make the scope complete, but volume alone does not teach. The guided sequence, comparisons and retrieval connect that source to useful practice. Random selection alone can miss a whole topic, so exam blueprints enforce section and objective coverage. A finite bank cannot predict the professor's exam, and repeated exposure can inflate scores; learners should explain answers aloud and retest later.

Schematic identification tests orientation and relationships, while the existing atlas and BI 231 test support spatial practice. They are complementary activities. Existing source-only accuracy holds remain visible; this expansion does not certify every 3D landmark as independently anatomically validated. No bone model geometry is changed in this curriculum update.

### Published muscle reference and final review

The optional atlas now contains all 669 components in the Z-Anatomy muscular-system export: 485 muscle parts and 184 supporting components, divided into five regional downloads. These counts describe source components, including separate heads and sides; they are not a claim of 485 distinct muscles or coverage of every human muscle. Original model coordinates are retained. Blender 4.5 backup snapshots precede every project save and model export. The source project is hash-verified as unchanged.

Muscle navigation supports side, regional colors, search, selected labels, head/part inspection, focus, surface peeling and restore. Static proximity is not proof of a certified attachment: exact attachment footprints, full dissection planes and moving-muscle rigs remain outside validated spatial assessment. Chapter 10 mechanisms instead use explicit qualitative tissue, sarcomere, activation and tension activities.

Final checks cover 33 sections, all 101 source objectives, the 50-question Chapter 10 form, the 150-question five-chapter form, retained four-chapter sessions, spelling distinctions, source identities and asset hashes.
