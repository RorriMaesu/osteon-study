# Spring course textbook figures

The numbered images are figures from *Anatomy and Physiology 2e*, OpenStax, Rice University, extracted from the course PDF. Images were cropped to the figure bounds and resized for the browser. Original anatomical labels are retained. The lesson viewer identifies the figure number, title, original source, and any additional credited contributors. [Section and figure index](../../src/ap3-figures.json)

Access for free at [openstax.org](https://openstax.org/details/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e). The supplied edition carries [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). Figures that include third-party material retain their original credit. The course uses the images for noncommercial education.
