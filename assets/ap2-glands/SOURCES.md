# Endocrine specimens

**cranial-glands.glb** contains the BodyParts3D hypothalamus, pineal body, pituitary gland, third ventricle (fluid space) and paired superior colliculi, in their native positions. The source and DBCLS license below apply. **ovary-v1.3.glb** is the unmodified left ovary surface from the Visible Human Female. This second donor’s organ opens independently. Kristen Browne and Heidi Schlehlein (2024), *3D Reference Organ for Ovary, Female, Left v1.3*. [Published model](https://doi.org/10.48539/HBM695.KCXS.294) · [Release metadata](https://cdn.humanatlas.io/digital-objects/ref-organ/ovary-female-left/v1.3/metadata.json) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The testicular region now also has a 3D specimen. Its section comparison uses the labeled OpenStax *Anatomy and Physiology 2e* [Fig. 27.4, anatomical section](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/27-1-anatomy-and-physiology-of-the-testicular-reproductive-system), converted to WebP.

**body-overview.glb** adds a single translucent orientation skeleton to the same native gland surfaces. The assembly combines the publisher’s aggregate skeletal system with separately indexed left and right limb bones, including fingers and toes. Only this skeletal context is decimated (15% target). The gland surfaces retain their source geometry. The 1.71 m head-to-foot extent was checked after export. In the regional locator and the overview, the patient’s left is positive X, superior is positive Y, and anterior is positive Z. The mesh positions are in meters.

**follicle-cutaway.glb** and **islet-cutaway.glb** are original three-dimensional teaching schematics created in Blender 4.5. The open thyroid follicle shows its epithelial wall, extracellular colloid, a neighboring parafollicular cell and a capillary. The islet illustrates intermingled alpha, beta and delta cells beside a capillary, with adjacent acinar clusters draining through ducts. Animated markers illustrate secretion and transport. Relative counts, spacing, color and movement speeds are illustrative. See the companion tissue micrographs from Figs. 17.12 and 17.18 and the [Blender source](../../scripts/build-ap2-gland-microanatomy.py).

**bodyparts-locator.glb** is a new Blender 4.5 assembly of native BodyParts3D 4.0 surfaces. The input is the `partof_BP3D_4.0_obj_99.zip` release and its `partof_element_parts.txt` index. The pituitary, sphenoid, two adrenals, kidneys, pancreas, thymus and skeletal landmarks retain their original relative positions. Blender converted millimeters to meters and exported glTF coordinates. The selected skeleton provides orientation in this specimen. The main Osteon atlas is a separate model. See [DBCLS downloads](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html) and the [reproducible Blender assembly](../../scripts/build-ap2-gland-locator.py).

BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International. Older OBJ headers also carry the original CC Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan notice. See the [publisher's February 2025 license](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html).

**pancreas-v1.3.glb** is the unmodified Human Reference Atlas organ from the National Library of Medicine’s Visible Human Male. It opens as a separate specimen. The author’s five labeled surfaces distinguish the head, uncinate process, neck, body and tail. Islets are taught with a separately scaled microscopic image. Kristen Browne and Heidi Schlehlein (2024), *3D Reference Organ for Pancreas, Male v1.3*, reviewed by Martha Campbell-Thompson. ## Regional specimens

**neck.glb** and **testicular-region.glb** contain evaluated, individually named meshes exported together in their source positions from the Z-Anatomy male atlas. The neck retains all four source parathyroids, the thyroid, trachea, hyoid and thyroid and cricoid cartilages. The testicular region retains both testes, epididymides and ducts, seminal glands, bladder, prostate and pubic symphysis. Materials are assigned for teaching. Osteon simplified the cortical, cerebellar, brainstem and skin surfaces for mobile viewing. Colors indicate selectable anatomy. BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science. The red marrow and skin/adipose cutaways are original teaching models built in Blender. Cell counts, sizes and spatial arrangements are illustrative. The marrow depicts one erythroblastic island and a nearby megakaryocyte. The skin block shows the epidermis, dermis and underlying adipose tissue. ## Inspectable sections of the pituitary and adrenal glands

The pituitary pathways and adrenal section are original Osteon teaching reconstructions. The pituitary model separates the anterior and posterior lobes and enlarges the axons and portal vessels. The adrenal model shows a cut surface through the capsule, three cortical zones, and medulla. Their shapes, scale, colors, and pathway timing are illustrative. The cranial gland, kidney, and adrenal surface locators retain their existing BodyParts3D source geometry.
