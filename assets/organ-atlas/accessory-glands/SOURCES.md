# Greater vestibular glands

The bulbourethral (Cowper’s) glands are covered by an illustrated reference in the organ search. The available male reconstruction did not resolve the ducts well enough to animate their outlets. Muro and colleagues show the gland and surrounding muscles in serial histological sections. [Human bulbourethral anatomy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8373982/) · [Independent review of the pelvic teaching reconstruction](https://www.unige.ch/ssi/application/files/5917/7158/4000/Memoire_Victor_Foubert.pdf)

The left and right glands and their ducts, vaginal wall, urethral submucosa and paired vestibular bulbs are from the CVH5 young-adult female pelvic reconstruction in Supporting Figure S2. The source authors reconstructed the anatomy from serial sections and smoothed the surfaces for teaching. These five surfaces share the original donor frame. [Wu and colleagues, *Interactive three-dimensional teaching models of the female and male pelvic floor*](https://doi.org/10.1002/ca.23508) · [open article and supplements](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7027585/) · [Figure S2](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC7027585.1/CA-33-275-s002.pdf).

Osteon extracted the published surfaces, oriented them together in Blender 4.5, assigned teaching colors and reduced the vaginal wall triangle count for browser display. Both gland surfaces retain the authors’ triangles. Each tracer follows the centers of successive cross sections of a duct and ends at the center of its open edge. Colors, tracers and playback timing are teaching additions.
