# Textbook illustrations

OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, J. Gordon Betts and contributing authors, © 2026 Rice University. From the user-supplied 2026 PDF edition. CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/ . Access for free at https://openstax.org/details/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e .

JPEG files retain the source illustrations. Browser SVG views crop them, mask labels and add numbered anatomical targets. These adaptations remain CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. `manifest.json` records original PDF and printed page numbers and image dimensions; `src/representations.js` records figure numbers, crops and targets. File `thoracic-rib.jpg` depicts thoracic rib articulations (figure 7.27). Atlas and axis views use `cervical.jpg` (figure 7.25).
