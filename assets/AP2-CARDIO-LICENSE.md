# Cardiac and vascular specimen credits

## Human Reference Atlas, HuBMAP

The heart dissection uses **3D Reference Organ for Heart, Male v1.3** by Kristen Browne, derived from the National Library of Medicine Visible Human Project. The coronary and great-vessel surfaces come from **3D Reference Organ for Blood Vasculature, Male v1.3** by Kristen Browne and Heidi Schlehlein. HuBMAP publishes both reference objects under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. [Heart record](https://doi.org/10.48539/HBM564.WKNG.249) · [Vasculature record](https://doi.org/10.48539/HBM473.XTPP.725) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Osteon used Blender 4.5 to select and combine the source surfaces in their original coordinates, simplify the trunk vessel mesh density and prepare material-neutral GLBs. The app supplies study colors. Conduction paths, the pericardial envelope and pressure-driven teaching leaflets are Osteon illustrations. The original four valve shapes and five papillary muscle groups appear in **Valves and supports**. The supplied source lacks independently labeled chordae. The four animated valves use independent left- and right-sided pressure gradients on one illustrative heartbeat. Their circumferences, hinge axes and leaflet motion are reconstructions placed at the native valve locations. In **Mitral supports**, pink papillary groups and the amber mitral surface are donor meshes. Four cyan strands join vertices on these structures to demonstrate their connections. Their paths, caliber and attachment sites are teaching assumptions. Compare the [University of Minnesota left ventricular papillary muscles](https://www.vhlab.umn.edu/atlas/left-ventricle/papillary-muscles/index.shtml).

## BodyParts3D

**BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science**, supplied the right lower-limb artery and bone geometry and the registered heart walls, sternum, ribs, costal cartilages and thoracic vertebrae. The 4.0 part-of archive and its FMA-to-element table were used together. DBCLS specifies CC BY 4.0 International on its current terms page. The downloaded 4.0 OBJ headers also carry an earlier CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan notice. Osteon additionally makes the two derivative GLBs available under that share-alike license. [Source data](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html) · [Current DBCLS terms](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.1/jp/)

Osteon converted millimeters to meters, grouped source elements under their anatomical names and assigned viewer colors. Distal foot elements keep their most specific source label when the source’s part-of hierarchy includes them under a larger vessel. The heart within the rib cage and the right-leg preparation each preserve a single donor’s spatial relationships. The detailed HRA heart dissection opens as a separate specimen.

## Regional limb circulation

**ap2-arm-circulation.glb** contains the usual right arch-to-hand arterial route and the major deep and superficial venous channels returning to the right atrium. **ap2-leg-venous-return.glb** contains the right plantar and dorsal venous arches, paired deep veins, great and small saphenous veins, iliac veins, both portions of the inferior vena cava and the right atrium. Same-specimen bones provide orientation. In the source segmentation the surface labeled anterior tibial veins includes the short proximal confluence up to the popliteal vein.

These are separate derivatives of Z-Anatomy, the libre 3D atlas of anatomy, by Gauthier Kervyn and contributors, based on BodyParts3D by The Database Center for Life Science. The original source curves and meshes were evaluated in Blender 4.5 and converted in their native world coordinates. Only the skeletal landmarks were simplified. Display colors and teaching routes are Osteon annotations. The regional files are licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). [Source model and authors](https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy) · [Regional export script](../scripts/build-ap2-limb-circulation.py).
